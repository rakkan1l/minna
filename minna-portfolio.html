About Me

A first-year student learning to read the mind.

I'm Ava, a first-year Psychology student who has always been drawn to the quiet questions behind people's behaviour — why we say what we say, feel what we feel, and remember what we remember.

My interest in psychology started with everyday observation: watching how people communicate differently under pressure, how emotions shape decisions, and how much of our behaviour is shaped by things we rarely notice. That curiosity is what brought me to this degree.

I'm still early in my studies, but I'm building a foundation in research methods, human behaviour, and critical thinking — and I'm genuinely excited to keep learning about the science behind how people understand themselves and each other.
